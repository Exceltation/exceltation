Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 28, 2026

Exceltation operates the Totus To-do mobile application (the "App") available on the Google Play Store. We value your privacy. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App, particularly regarding ads, premium payment features, and synchronization tools.

1. Information We Collect and Process

We do not personally operate external data storage servers or sell, rent, or trade your data. However, the App relies on trusted third-party services that collect data to perform essential core functions:

  • Google Account & Cloud Sync: If you use the backup or sync features, the App requests permission to authenticate using your Google Account. This grants the App secure, direct access to your isolated Google Drive storage sandbox solely to store, fetch, and synchronize your local to-do database file. We do not read, store, or access other files in your cloud storage.
  • Local Device Storage: If you use the App entirely offline, your tasks, lists, and other data entries stay securely inside your device's local internal application container.

2. Third-Party Services and Advertising

To keep basic features available for free, the free tier of our App incorporates third-party advertising networks. These networks collect tracking and analytical metrics directly via their software development kits (SDKs) embedded in the app binary:

  • Google AdMob (Non-Intrusive Banner Only): The Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects system metrics, including your device's unique IP address (to calculate a rough global location), basic interactive taps, app crash analytics, and persistent hardware identifiers like your Android Advertising ID (AAID). Google leverages this data to combat traffic fraud, run network debugging, and serve advertisements. Our App strictly limits ads to a single, small visual banner positioned at the bottom of the screen. We do not use intrusive full-screen pop-ups, video ads, or interstitial interruptions.
  • Premium Ad-Free Experience: If you choose to purchase the Premium Upgrade, all advertisements are completely disabled, and the third-party advertising tracking frameworks cease processing your data for ad-delivery purposes.

3. Payment Services & Paid Features

The App offers a single, one-time lifetime purchase option to remove advertisements and permanently unlock all core premium features. There are no additional microtransactions, recurring subscription elements, or ongoing fees. This transaction is executed directly through the Google Play Billing system. We never view, collect, or store your credit card details, billing addresses, or processing credentials. Google provides us solely with anonymized digital purchase tokens to verify your active lifetime premium license.

4. Data Retention, Control, and Erasure

You keep full control over your software data environment:

  • Opt-Out from Tracking: On the free tier, you can completely disable personalized ad tracking at any time by visiting your device parameters (Settings > Privacy > Ads) and choosing to reset or turn off your advertising identifier.
  • Data Deletion: Uninstalling the App or clearing its storage container through your Android system settings will permanently drop all local application data. To disconnect cloud synchronization data, you can remove your Google Account and select the relevant option inside the App or revoke the App's access tokens via your Google Account Security Dashboard.

5. Regional Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your location, you may have rights under regional data protection laws (such as the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation and the California Consumer Privacy Act). These include the right to access, update, or request the deletion of data processed by our third-party partners. Because we do not store your data on our own infrastructure, you can exercise these rights directly through your device settings or Google Account configurations as outlined in Section 4.

6. Children's Privacy

Our services do not target and are not intended for children under the age of 13 (or the applicable legal age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child under the age of 13 has provided data through our third-party SDK partners, please contact us via the email below so we can work with our partners to promptly remove it.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may refresh our terms from time to time. We will indicate any policy changes by deploying the revised copy to this page and updating the effective date at the top.
