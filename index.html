Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions, answered.

Is this a one-time purchase? +
Yes. Once you purchase a product, it’s yours for life. We do not have any products with subscriptions or recurring charges. And, you are entitled to any future versions released for the product(s) you purchased.
Do I need Excel skills to use these products? +
No. But, you will need to enable certain features for certain products.
Will this work with my Excel? +
All of Exceltation’s Excel and other MSO products are built with MSO 365 (the latest version of Excel), unless otherwise specified. If you do not have the latest version of Excel, your product(s) may not work properly, fully, or at all.
Do I need Macros to use Excel Products? +
If the product is a macro enabled workbook (.xlsm/.docm) then yes, otherwise (.xlsx/.docx) no. Make sure to enable these features by option Excel or Word then going to File > Options > Trust Center > Trust Center Settings… > Macro Settings > Then check the boxes “Enable all macros” and “Trust access to the VBA project object model”.
Can I share my purchased workbook with people I know? +
Not entirely. Exceltation understands that we have no control over what you do after purchase, regarding sharing a product via your e-mail, share drive, or such as. Especially since many of our products are not proprietary, thus there is no product licensing. However, re-selling our intellectual property under you or your business’ name is prohibited under copyright law.
